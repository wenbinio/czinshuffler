# What is a reload, how it works and what are the ways to get it?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882125-what-is-a-reload-how-it-works-and-what-are-the-ways-to-get-it
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

Reloads are available to players once they reach Sapphire rank and remain available as they rank up.  
​

Reloads give you a fixed amount of rewards, which can be claimed at specific intervals.  
​

**How Do Reloads Work?**  
​

Reloads are bonuses awarded based on your play activity.  
​

**Important** : If you miss a reload claim, it will expire—so be sure to log in and claim it! Each reload claim expires after 24 hours.  
​

**How to Claim Your Reload Bonus**  
​

Once you reach **Sapphire** , you’ll unlock **weekly reloads**! Just contact your **VIP Host** to choose a plan with either:

  * **1 claim per day** , or

  * **3 claims per day**
