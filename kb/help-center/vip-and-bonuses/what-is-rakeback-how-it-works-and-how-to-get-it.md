# What is Rakeback, how it works and how to get it?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882127-what-is-rakeback-how-it-works-and-how-to-get-it
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

**Rakeback unlocks at Bronze 1.**

Every game has a house edge—commonly called the “rake.” With Rakeback, you get a percentage of that edge back, giving you real value on every play, win or lose.

You’ll always earn a **fixed percentage** based on your play, and **Rakeback applies to all games** —from slots and table games to live dealer games. It also works on both Gold Coins (GC) and Shuffle Cash (SC).

**How It Works**

You’ll receive **5% of the house edge back** , based on how much you play.

**Example** :

If you play with 1,000 **Shuffle Cash** on a game with a **2% house edge** , the total rake would be 20 Shuffle Cash. With 5% Rakeback, you'd get 1 **Shuffle Cash** back—win or lose.

The more you play, the more Rakeback you can earn!
