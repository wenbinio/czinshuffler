# Shuffle.us Affiliate Program

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12174023-shuffle-us-affiliate-program
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

The Shuffle.us Affiliate Program boasts some of the highest affiliate rates in the industry and provides assets to help you get started on your earning journey. In this article we'll cover the details of the program.  
  
You can access your Affiliate Program page [here](https://shuffle.us/affiliate/overview).

**Calculating Affiliate Earnings**

Below is the formula used to calculate your earnings. Please keep in mind different games have different theoretical edge and earnings are calculated after bonuses and promotions are given to a player.  
​  
The default affiliate commission rate is currently 10%.  
​
    
    
    (Played Amount * Theoretical Edge * Affiliate Rate as a Percentage)/2

  
​**Payout Frequency**

Payouts are calculated in real time. Please note that you may need to refresh your page to view the updated change.  
​  
Please note that payouts are not issued to your balance directly, you can claim them on your Affiliate Earnings page [here](https://shuffle.us/affiliate/earnings).

Affiliate commissions are not subject to any playthrough requirement. However you must still meet minimum redemption requirements if you would like to redeem your Shuffle Cash for prizes.

**Creating Multiple Campaigns**

You can create multiple campaigns via the campaigns tab within the affiliate program area of Shuffle.us.   
​  
From there you can view your current campaigns and create a new one with custom referral codes and names.  
​  
Please note that you're not able to delete campaigns once they have been created.

  
By creating multiple campaigns, you can effectively target and engage with your audience.
