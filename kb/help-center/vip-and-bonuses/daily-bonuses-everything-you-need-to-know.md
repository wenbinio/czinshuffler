# Daily Bonuses – everything you need to know

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12173977-daily-bonuses-everything-you-need-to-know
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

The daily bonus is issued to all players everyday **at 5 AM UTC or 1 AM EST** and is available to players of all ranks, the only requirement is that you have completed level 1 verification.  
​

Bonuses appear under the "Wallet" button and then "[Daily Bonus](https://shuffle.us/vip-program?md-tab=Daily+Bonus&modal=wallet)".   
​  
You can head to your Daily Bonus tab to see how much you'll earn that day from it!

Keep your streak up in order to increase the amount of bonus you receive each day!  
​  
After 7 days, your daily bonus amount will be 0.4 SC and 25,000 GC, it wont reset as long as you keep your streak up!
