# How to recover your account

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882143-how-to-recover-your-account
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

We understand that losing access to your email address can be a challenging experience. To begin the email change process, please contact our team at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#bccfc9ccccd3cec8fccfd4c9dadad0d992c9cf) from the new email address you wish to link to your account, and we will initiate the account recovery procedure.

When contacting our team, please be ready to provide any information they may request from you. We value your security and appreciate your cooperation in ensuring a smooth process.   
​  
Please allow up to 24 hours for our initial reply as we diligently work to assist you in resolving this matter.

Should you have any further inquiries or require additional assistance, please do not hesitate to reach out. We are here to help.
