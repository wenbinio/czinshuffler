## TL;DR of the platform

- **Model**: dual-currency sweepstakes casino — Gold Coins (fun, no value) + Shuffle Cash (won SC redeems at $1/SC; $100 minimum; 1x playthrough; never purchasable).
- **Operator**: Munyon Canyon Ltd (BVI); US mail address PO Box 9461, Portland ME 04104. Sister brand of Shuffle.com (Natural Nine B.V., Curaçao-licensed crypto casino).
- **Launch**: September 2025. Casino-only, ~500–600 games, in-house Originals, per-game edge disclosed, provably-fair system.
- **Money flows**: Fiat mode (cards/ACH; $9k/day purchase cap; 100k SC/24h redemption cap; ACH ~1 business day) or Crypto mode (14+ assets, no caps). Redemption rail must match your most recent purchase mode; Level-2 KYC to redeem. Zero purchase fees.
- **Bonuses**: 25,000 GC + 1 SC welcome; daily bonus at 5AM UTC plateauing at 0.4 SC + 25k GC with a maintained streak; AMOE postcards worth 2 SC each; VIP ladder (Wood → Diamond 5 at 37M XP) with 5% rakeback from Bronze 1.
- **Availability**: most US states — but the Sweepstakes Rules and the help center **disagree** on FL/GA/HI/LA/MD (see legal file). Age 18+/majority.
