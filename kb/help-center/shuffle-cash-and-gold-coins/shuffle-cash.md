# Shuffle Cash

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882134-shuffle-cash
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

Shuffle Cash (SC) is a special type of reward available to players on Shuffle.us. You can receive it in two main ways:

  * Through daily logins  
​

  * As a bonus included with select Gold Coin bundles  
​

Once added to your account, Shuffle Cash can be redeemed for specific prizes, including certain cryptocurrencies. However, redemptions are subject to minimum redemption amounts and verification processes.

If you receive free Shuffle Cash as part of a bundle purchase, it must be played through at least once before it becomes eligible for redemption. This ensures fair use and maintains a secure experience for all players.  
​  
For example, if you receive 5 SC as part of your bundle purchase, you must play at least 5 SC worth of games before being able to redeem it.

Shuffle Cash has a redemption rate of 1 SC = $1 worth of prizes.

For more information on playthrough and verification requirements, or if you have questions about your Shuffle Cash balance, feel free to reach out to our support team — we're here 24/7.
