# Supported crypto assets and chains

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882069-supported-crypto-assets-and-chains
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

Shuffle.us supports a range of assets for purchasing & redeeming including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tron, USDT, USDC and more!  
​

We currently support the following assets:

  * Bitcoin (BTC)

  * Ethereum (ETH, BEP20, BASE)

  * Litecoin (LTC)

  * USDT (ERC20, BEP20, TRC20, POL)

  * USDC (ERC20, BEP20, SOL, POL, ARB, BASE)

  * Tron (TRX)

  * POL (Polygon, ETH)

  * Ripple (XRP)

  * Solana (SOL)

  * Dogecoin (DOGE)

  * Binance Coin (BNB)

  * Avalanche (AVAX)

  * Toncoin (TON)

  * Shiba Inu (ERC20)

  * Bonk (SOL)

  * dogwifhat WIF (SOL)

  * TRUMP (SOL)

  * PUMP (SOL)

  * DAI (ERC20)
