## Customer Care Principles

At Shuffle.us, we strive to provide a safe environment for our players. We actively encourage and promote responsible gameplay practices, and provide tools to assist our users in maintaining control of their gameplay. 

### Tips for Players

  * Make sure that the decision to play on Shuffle.us is your own personal choice.

  * Avoid social gameplay while upset, emotional, or under the influence of drugs, alcohol, or any other intoxicating substance that may impair your cognitive abilities. 

  * Take frequent breaks during your social gameplay session.

  * Determine how much you will spend before playing.

  * Before you start playing, familiarize yourself with the rules of the games as well as the Responsible Play options in the Shuffle Wise section of Shuffle.us.

  * Keep track of time spent playing.

  * Purchase Gold Coins only with money that you can afford to spend. 

  * Set and stick to a budget for your entertainment.

  * Set a time limit before playing.

  * Remember that the results of each game are random. You cannot predict when you are going to win.

  * Do not prioritize social gaming over your daily life and responsibilities.

  * Remember that playing online social games is a form of entertainment and should not be considered a way to make money.

### We suggest that you don't play on our platform if:

  * It interferes with your daily responsibilities

  * You are being treated or are in recovery for any type of dependency

  * You are under the influence of alcohol or any other substance

  * Your primary purpose is to pay debts using prizes you might win

  * You are currently experiencing financial difficulty or a traumatic life event

  * You do not understand how to play the games provided on the platform

  * You have any mental health concerns, cognitive impairment, or brain injury

### Self-Assessment

We invite you to ask yourself the below questions; the more you answer ‘yes,’ the more likely you may be developing a problem gaming issue and should consider self-excluding yourself from using our platform: If you feel you have a social gaming problem then we strongly advise you seek help immediately. Admitting you have a problem is not a sign of weakness – it is the first step to reclaiming your life. 

  1. Do you prefer to stay away from work or family due to social gaming?

  2. Have you had repeated failed attempts to stop or control social gaming?

  3. Have you ever borrowed money to play social games?

  4. Do you play social games to escape worry or trouble?

  5. Do you at times neglect caring for yourself or family in order to play social games?

### Tips for Parents

With the internet accessible via mobile devices and in homes around the world, responsible gaming relies heavily on parental control and supervision. To ensure child safety on the internet, we encourage our customers to make use of filtering software to prevent minors from accessing inappropriate online material.

  * Keep your username and password safe.

  * Do not leave your computer or device unattended when logged in to your account.

  * Do not use the "Remember Me" feature on a shared computer or device, especially if you share your computer or device with children.

  * Take particular care regarding the use by minors of all electronic and mobile devices.

  * Keep your bank cards and bank account details out of reach of children.

  * Do not allow minors (under 18 years or the age of majority in your jurisdiction) to participate in any gaming activity on Shuffle.us.

  * Make use of filtering software to prevent your children from accessing inappropriate material online.

If you share your device or computer with friends or family who are under the legal age to participate in online social gaming, we recommend that you monitor your account, restrict their access to gaming platforms to ensure the account cannot be accessed by underage individuals.
