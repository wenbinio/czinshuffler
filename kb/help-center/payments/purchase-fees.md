# Purchase fees

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/14808971-purchase-fees
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

Shuffle.us takes **zero** fees across all purchases, whether they be via crypto or via fiat.  
​  
If you are charged any foreign transactions or FX fees, this is imposed by the payment intermediaries involved during the purchase process.  
​  
To avoid such fees, please try using a different payment method or use a different card.
