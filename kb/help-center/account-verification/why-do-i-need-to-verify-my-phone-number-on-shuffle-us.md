# Why do I need to verify my phone number on Shuffle.us?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882040-why-do-i-need-to-verify-my-phone-number-on-shuffle-us
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

Verifying your phone number is an important step in creating a secure and reliable account with Shuffle.us. It helps confirm your identity, protect your account, and ensure you can always recover access if needed.  
​

**Why Phone Verification Matters**

  1. **Account Security  
​**Phone verification adds a layer of protection to help prevent unauthorized access to your account.  
​

  2. **Access Recovery  
​**If you ever need to recover your account, a verified phone number makes the process faster and more secure.

**Secondary Security Layer**

Along with your email, your phone number provides an additional safeguard for sensitive account changes.
