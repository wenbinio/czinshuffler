# How do tournaments work?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12468362-how-do-tournaments-work
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

You will find tournaments posted on the carousel of the home page of Shuffle.us.  
​  
They track play progress and eligibility live, and prizes are paid out automatically as soon as a tournament completes.  
​  
Tournament Formats  
​

**Cumulative Multiplier** : Earn points by achieving specific multipliers on qualifying games. Points are added to a leaderboard, ranking players for prizes.

Example: Hit 100x on games to earn 1 point; the total points determine ranking and rewards.

**Qualifier** : Complete target multipliers across multiple games to qualify for a shared prize.

Example: Hit 40x on Sugar Rush, 80x on Big Bass, and 120x on Le Bandit to share a prize pool.

**Max Multiplier** : Compete to achieve the highest multipliers on specific games.

Example: Top 50 players hitting the highest multipliers on Gates of Olympus 1000 split the prize pool.

**Max Payout** : Compete for the largest payouts on qualifying games.

Example: Top 50 players achieving the highest payouts on Sweet Bonanza share the prize pool.

**Races** : Wager the highest total amount in designated game groups to win.

Example: players competing in a race for the highest total wagered within the game group over a specific period.
