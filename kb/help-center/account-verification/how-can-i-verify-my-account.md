# How can I verify my account?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882034-how-can-i-verify-my-account
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

Verifying your identity on Shuffle.us is simple and secure, thanks to our state of the art verification software. The process is quick—just have a valid form of ID ready, such as a driver’s licence or national ID card, and a proof of address document, such as a utility bill.  
​

Head [here](https://shuffle.us/settings/verify) in order to complete the verification steps.  
​  
There are three levels of verification on Shuffle.us.  
​  
​**Level 1 Verification - Phone verification and declaring your information**

  * You'll need to complete Level 1 Verification in order to make purchases and play on Shuffle.us.

**Level 2 Verification - Identity Verification**

  * Completing Level 2 Verification will allow you to redeem prizes. Once you submit level 2 verification, you won't be able to edit your Level 1 verification.

**Level 3 Verification - Proof of Address Verification**

You’ll be asked to submit a photo of your ID placed on a flat surface to complete Level 2 Verification. Our verification software will prompt you in case it needs more info from you.

We can’t accept temporary IDs or photocopies at this time.  
​

If you run into any issues, our support team is here and happy to help. Most people complete verification in just a few minutes, and you’ll be playing in no time.
