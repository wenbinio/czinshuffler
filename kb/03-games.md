## ⚠️ Sister-brand ambiguity (read first)

Shuffle.us (US sweepstakes casino, GC/SC) and Shuffle.com (international real-money crypto casino) are sister brands sharing branding and UI. **Many review sites conflate the two**, quoting Shuffle.com's totals (10,000–15,000+ games, 56 providers) for Shuffle.us. Treat any large game-count claim with suspicion; figures below are flagged by which site they were verified against.
