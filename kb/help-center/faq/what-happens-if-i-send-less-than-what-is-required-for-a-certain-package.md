# What happens if I send less than what is required for a certain package?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/13626642-what-happens-if-i-send-less-than-what-is-required-for-a-certain-package
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

That's totally ok! The figures displayed on the "Buy Coins" page are visual only, if you end up sending less or more than what a certain package shows, you'll still be credited GC and your free SC if you purchased a bundle with SC.  
​  
It will be credited at a rate of $1 = 10,000 GC and 1 free SC.
