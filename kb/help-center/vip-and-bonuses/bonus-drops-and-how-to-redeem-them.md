# Bonus drops and how to redeem them

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882126-bonus-drops-and-how-to-redeem-them
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

Bonus drops are one of the several types of rewards we provide at Shuffle.us. At random times each day, we drop codes in our Telegram channel.  
​  
There is only one official Shuffle.us Telegram channel at this time.  
​

You can find bonus drops here:

  * **[Shuffle.us Main Telegram Channel](https://t.me/shuffleus)**

**How to Redeem a Bonus Drop**

  1. Hover over or click your profile picture.

  2. Click the “[Redeem Code](https://shuffle.us/settings/account?modal=redeem)” button.

  3. Enter the code from the Telegram drop.

Make sure to meet the **playthrough requirement** listed with each drop on Telegram—and be quick! Drops are highly sought after, and only the fastest fingers get to claim them.  
​
