# How to calculate your VIP progress?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882122-how-to-calculate-your-vip-progress
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

Playing using **Shuffle Cash (SC)** or **Gold Coins (GC)** generates XP! 

  * 1 Shuffle Cash played = 1 XP

  * 2,000,000 Gold Coins played = 1 XP

You can track how much XP you’ve earned in your account's lifetime at your Settings page [here](https://shuffle.us/settings/account).  
​  
This article only details how much playthrough is needed to reach a certain level. If you would like to find out about each level's bonus, you can find that in this [article](https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12174032-shuffle-us-vip-level-up-bonuses).
