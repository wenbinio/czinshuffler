# How do challenges work?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12520237-how-do-challenges-work
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

You can find challenges posted here:  
​  
​<https://shuffle.us/challenges>  
​  
These are bounties that are automatically credited to the **first** person that hits them!

Please note that certain challenges are only visible to players under certain affiliate codes.  
​  
Also, while you may choose to use bonus buys to hit challenges, we would advise against doin so as it makes it **significantly** more difficult to do so.
