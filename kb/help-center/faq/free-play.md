# Free Play

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/13067374-free-play
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

On Shuffle.us, you can play any of our Shuffle Originals with 0 SC or 0 GC if you wish to get a feel for the game.  
​  
Please note that there is a 24 hour limit on the number of free plays you can place, at this time the limit is not public. This resets at 5AM UTC each day.
