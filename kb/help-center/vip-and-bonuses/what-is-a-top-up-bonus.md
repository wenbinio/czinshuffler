# What is a Top Up Bonus?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12235988-what-is-a-top-up-bonus
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

Every 6 hours, you can claim a free top up of 2,500 Gold Coins (GC)! Just click on the "Wallet" button and then on "[Top Up](https://shuffle.us/?md-tab=Top+Up&modal=wallet)".
