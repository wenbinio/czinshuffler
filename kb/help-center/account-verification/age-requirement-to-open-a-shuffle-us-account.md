# Age Requirement to open a Shuffle.us account

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882041-age-requirement-to-open-a-shuffle-us-account
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

As stated in our Terms of Service, our platform is designated solely for players who are over 18 years of age or the minimum legal age of majority whichever is higher in the jurisdiction in which you are located at the time of accessing or using the Service.

Thus, minors under this age are not permitted to use our services.

For Underage Account Holders

  * **If you opened your account before reaching the relevant legal age, you will be granted access to your account upon coming of age.**  
​

  * **To proceed once you meet the age requirement, please contact our support team at[[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#34474144445b464074475c41525258511a4147) from the email address registered with your account. Our team will assist you throughout the process.**

For further details, kindly review Section 3.1 of our comprehensive Terms and Conditions, available here: [Terms and Conditions](https://shuffle.us/info/terms).

If you have any questions or need additional support, please feel free to contact us. We're here to assist you.
