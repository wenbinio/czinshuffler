# Lost Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882149-lost-two-factor-authentication-2fa
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

If you lost access to your 2FA code, please contact us from the email linked to your account and message [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#b5c6c0c5c5dac7c1f5c6ddc0d3d3d9d09bc0c6), we will initiate a 2FA recovery procedure.

* * *

We will ask you to provide us with some information related to that account, and once we have reviewed and verified it, you will be able to access your account again.
