# Vault

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12253714-vault
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

The Vault is a separate balance from your wallet. When you transfer funds into your vault, it will cannot be used for games.  
​

You can find your Vault [here](https://shuffle.us/?modal=vault).
