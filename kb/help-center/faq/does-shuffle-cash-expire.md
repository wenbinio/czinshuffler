# Does Shuffle Cash Expire?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12264492-does-shuffle-cash-expire
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

**IMPORTANT** : Shuffle Cash expires after 60 days since a player has last played. So if someone is inactive for 60 days, their SC will disappear.
