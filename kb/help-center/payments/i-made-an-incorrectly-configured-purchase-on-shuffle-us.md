# I made an incorrectly configured purchase on Shuffle.us

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12855667-i-made-an-incorrectly-configured-purchase-on-shuffle-us
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

If you have made an incorrectly configured purchase, please reach out to support in Live Chat or via [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#53202623233c212713203b2635353f367d2620)

Our team will do their best to assist but we cannot guarantee retrieval.   
​  
For this, please review our policy around wrong deposits and retrieval.

**The minimum retrieval USD amount must be over $10. Please ensure to use the right coin/chain when making a purchase.  
​**

**Wrong deposit for coins that Shuffle.us supports but sent on the wrong chain that Shuffle.us supports, missing destination tag, etc:**  
​

  * The first occurrence will be retrieved with no penalty

  * Future occurrences will incur a 10% penalty, up to $20 on the purchase's USD amount

**Wrong purchases for coins that Shuffle.us does not support and/or chains that Shuffle.us does not support:  
​**

  * Our team will require time to investigate case by case

  * If the coin is retrievable, depending on the coin/chain, there can be a penalty for up to 50% of the deposit amount

  * If the coin is not retrievable, we will not be able to credit the deposit at all

**Ripple (XRP) and The Open Network (TON) deposits with missing destination tag or memo  
​**

  * Please reach out to support if you made a Ripple or TON purchase without a destination tag / memo and our team will investigate whether this can be recovered.

  * To avoid this, please ensure to include a destination tag or memo.
