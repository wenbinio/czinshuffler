# Exchanging Gold Coins for Shuffle Cash

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882138-exchanging-gold-coins-for-shuffle-cash
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

At Shuffle.us, all gameplay takes place using virtual token currencies — specifically Gold Coins and Shuffle Cash. Here’s a breakdown of what each token is and how it works:

## **Gold Coins**

Gold Coins are a virtual currency used purely for entertainment purposes on Shuffle.us. They have no monetary value and:

  * Cannot be redeemed, refunded, or exchanged for real-world currency  
​

  * Are intended for gameplay only  
​

  * Are not linked to any prizes or cash-outs  
​

Gold Coins provide a way to enjoy the games on our platform in a risk-free, non-redeemable environment.

## **Shuffle Cash**

Shuffle Cash is another form of virtual token currency on the site, but with some important differences:

  * Shuffle Cash cannot be purchased — it is only awarded through free promotional offers, such as daily logins or selected bundle bonuses.  
​

  * While it also holds no direct monetary value, Shuffle Cash can be redeemed for eligible prizes, including cryptocurrency, provided that all sweeping and verification requirements are met.
