# Linking a bank account to Shuffle.us

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/13626697-linking-a-bank-account-to-shuffle-us
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

If you haven’t linked a bank account to your account yet, you can do so by clicking on the [wallet](https://shuffle.us/?md-tab=Purchase+Coins&modal=wallet) button and choosing “Bank Account” as your payment method. The prompts on screen will guide you through linking your bank account.
