# Monthly Bonuses - everything you need to know

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882123-monthly-bonuses-everything-you-need-to-know
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

The monthly bonus is issued to eligible players on the **first Friday of each month at 4AM UTC** , starting from**Silver 1**.  
​

Bonuses will appear on your **[VIP Page](https://shuffle.us/vip-program)**. If you don’t see a bonus there, it may mean you're not currently eligible.  
​  
​**When does the monthly bonus expire?**

The monthly bonus expires after the next monthly bonus has been issued.
