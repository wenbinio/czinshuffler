# Where can I find my leaderboard prize?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/13067395-where-can-i-find-my-leaderboard-prize
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

If you're under an affiliate that has a leaderboard and manage to win a prize, one of our team members will issue you your prize directly into your balance.  
​  
Please note that this only applies to affiliate leaderboards, challenges and onsite tournaments pay out prizes instantly upon completion and you can claim those prizes here:  
​  
​<https://shuffle.us/vip-program>  
​  
​Please allow several days after an affiliate leaderboard has ended for our team to process prizes.
