# How are the weekly and monthly bonuses calculated?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12173970-how-are-the-weekly-and-monthly-bonuses-calculated
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

The weekly and monthly bonuses are calculated based on your:

  * Played amount since the previous weekly/monthly bonus

  * House edge of the games you played since the previous weekly/monthly bonus

  * Profit values since the previous weekly/monthly bonus

This is among other factors that are taken into account. Unfortunately we are not able to estimate the size of your bonus in advance.

For your first weekly or monthly bonus ever, it would consider your activity since the previous weekly or monthly bonus.
