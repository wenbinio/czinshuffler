# How to redeem rewards in cryptocurrency?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882140-how-to-redeem-rewards-in-cryptocurrency
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

Once you have obtained enough Shuffle Cash to meet the minimum redemption amount, navigate to Wallet > [Redeem](https://shuffle.us/settings/verify?md-tab=Redeem&modal=wallet) to redeem your prize.  
​  
Minimum redemption amounts will vary based on the currency you are redeeming in and will be displayed on the page when attempting to redeem.  
​  
Please note that you must have completed level 2 verification in order to redeem prizes in crypto. You can do so [here](https://shuffle.us/settings/verify).

On the Redeem Modal, you will be asked to enter your cryptocurrency address as well as the amount of Shuffle Cash you wish to Redeem via Crypto.  
​  
Once approved, redemptions are usually instant! They might take longer if the network you've chosen to redeem your prize on is experiencing delays.  
​  
You can find the status of your [redemption](https://shuffle.us/transactions/redemptions) here.

**Important note** : You can only redeem your rewards in the cryptocurrency you used for your previous purchase. 

If you have set up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on your account, enter the active code and click the Redeem button.

If you have not set up 2FA yet, you will receive a code in your email which you will need to enter in the corresponding field in order to successfully proceed with redemption.
