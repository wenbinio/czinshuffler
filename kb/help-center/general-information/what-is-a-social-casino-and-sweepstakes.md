# What is a Social Casino and Sweepstakes?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882020-what-is-a-social-casino-and-sweepstakes
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

A social casino is an online platform that offers casino-style games for entertainment only—no real money is involved. Instead of wagering cash, players use virtual tokens such as Gold Coins and Shuffle Cash to enjoy the experience.

**Key Features** :

  * Game Variety: Play popular games like slots, roulette, and blackjack using Gold Coins or Shuffle Cash.

  * Interactive Experience: Social casinos are designed to be community-focused, allowing players to connect with friends, share achievements, and participate in vibrant online communities.

  * Entertainment-Focused: The goal is fun and social interaction, not financial reward. All games are free to play, with additional value provided through features like daily bonuses and rewards.

By offering a safe, engaging, and socially-driven experience, social casinos provide a fun and risk-free alternative to traditional online casinos.
