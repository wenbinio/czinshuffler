# Why was the document I used for verification rejected?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882039-why-was-the-document-i-used-for-verification-rejected
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

Your verification may have encountered an issue for a number of reasons.

Verification documents may be rejected if they are **expired**. Please ensure that you submit valid, up-to-date documents issued in the United States.

Additionally, only specific types of identification are supported for verification purposes—namely, an **ID card** or **driver’s license**. Unfortunately, other forms of identification are not accepted. We appreciate your understanding.

**Now, a few other possible causes:**

If the images of your ID or selfie were **unclear** or **blurry** , the system may have been unable to process them. We recommend retaking and submitting clearer images.

Please also ensure that your ID is undamaged and that all text and numbers are fully legible.

In such cases, please contact our support team at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#f5868085859a8781b5869d8093939990db8086). They will assist you in accessing your original account, as our Terms of Service do not allow duplicate accounts.
