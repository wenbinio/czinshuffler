# Redemption limits and requirements

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12277636-redemption-limits-and-requirements
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

At Shuffle.us, there are a couple requirements for redemptions:  
​

  * You must have completed at least 1x play through on any Shuffle Cash (SC) received as part of a Gold Coin (GC) bundle purchase.

  * Players from Florida are limited to a redemption cap of 5,000 SC a day. This resets each day at 5AM UTC, or 1AM ET.

  * Players from other states are not subject to a redemption cap.

  * You may only redeem prizes in the currency you used for your **most recent purchase.**

  * You can only redeem prizes via bank transfer if you are in Fiat mode and only in crypto if you are in Crypto mode.

  * If you would like to redeem using card or bank transfer, you must have completed level 2 verification.

  * There is a 100 SC minimum required for fiat redemptions.

  * At this time, there is a 100,000 SC redemption limit on a rolling 24 hour limit for fiat redemptions. Crypto redemptions do not have any limits.
