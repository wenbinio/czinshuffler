# Why was my purchase or redemption declined?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/13626712-why-was-my-purchase-or-redemption-declined
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

As outlined in our Terms & Conditions, all payment methods used for purchasing Gold Coin packages must be legally and beneficially owned by you. Payment methods must be registered in **your own legal name**.

Purchases can be declined if your information has been entered incorrectly. Please ensure the following information is correct when linking your bank account:  
​

  * Name

  * Billing Address

  * CVV

  * AVS

  * OTP or security question from your card provider or bank

We only support US based cards for purchases.  
​

## I am unable to make a redemption to my Bank

Shuffle.us supports redemptions to most major U.S. banks; however, we do not currently support redemptions to certain online-only banks, including, but not limited to:

  * Chime

  * Cash App

  * Varo

  * Dave

  * Revolut

## What can I do if my card purchase declined?  

If your card purchase was declined, please contact your card issuer or bank. You may also try waiting 15 minutes before trying again.  
​  
If that still doesn't work, that means that unfortunately our payment provider is unable to accept your transaction, we can't make any changes to this manually.

## Which payment channels are supported for card purchases?

At this time, [Shuffle.us](http://shuffle.us) supports Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
