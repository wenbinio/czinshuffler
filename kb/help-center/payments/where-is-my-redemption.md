# Where is my redemption?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/13717074-where-is-my-redemption
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

Bank transfer redemptions generally are resolved within the same day as they are initiated. In most cases, you can expect to receive the funds within a few hours.   
​  
However, sometimes it can take 1 to 2 business days, particularly if initiated near or during a weekend, as redemptions are processed only on business days. Redemptions typically do not complete on Saturdays or other non-business days.   
​  
If it hasn't reached your account after 3 business days, please reach out to us at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#c6b5b3b6b6a9b4b286b5aeb3a0a0aaa3e8b3b5) with details on your redemptions. Additionally, a "confirmed" status indicates that Shuffle.us has successfully processed your request on its end.
