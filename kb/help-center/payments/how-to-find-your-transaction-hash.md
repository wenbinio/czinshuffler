# How to find your transaction hash

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882062-how-to-find-your-transaction-hash
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

If your recent crypto purchase hasn’t appeared in your Shuffle.us account yet, don’t worry — our support team can help.

## **What Is a Transaction Hash?**

A**transaction hash** (sometimes called a “TXID”) is a unique string of letters and numbers that identifies a specific crypto transaction on the blockchain.  
​

Example:

_0xf2081e7f80af154b8659f26df72c8d8f7f5129aa090f130046fefedec027e5b0  
​_

Or it might appear as a link to a blockchain explorer like Etherscan.   
​

 _<https://etherscan.io/tx/> _  
​

Once you've got that, go ahead and reach out to our Shuffle.us support team for further assistance.
