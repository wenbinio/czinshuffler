# Understanding Your Account’s Purchase Limit

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11911390-understanding-your-account-s-purchase-limit
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

To help provide a safe and secure environment for all players, every account on Shuffle.us is subject to a daily purchase limit, as outlined in our Terms of Service.  
​

## **What Is My Purchase Limit?**

Your daily maximum purchase amount is set at $9,000.00 USD with Fiat.  
​  
There are **no** limits with crypto.  
​

This limit applies to Gold Coin purchases made within a 24-hour rolling period.

## **What happens If I purchase over my limit?**

If your purchase goes over the $9,000.00 limit, the excess amount will be applied over the following days, up to the limit.

## **How Can I Track My Limit?**

You can view your current usage and the time until your limit resets by visiting the Buy Gold Coins section in your wallet. 

It updates in real time, helping you manage your purchases easily and transparently.
