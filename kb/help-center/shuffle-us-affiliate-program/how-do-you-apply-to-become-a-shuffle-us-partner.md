# How do you apply to become a Shuffle.us Partner?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12238849-how-do-you-apply-to-become-a-shuffle-us-partner
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

**Become a Shuffle Partner today! Reach out to our partnerships team to get started!**

  
If you are interested in joining Shuffle.us as a partner, please send your information to [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#d2a2b3a0a6bcb7a0a1babba2a192a1baa7b4b4beb7fca7a1) with answers to the following:

  * Can you share links to your owned pages that you'll be using to promote Shuffle.us?

  * What are the stats of your owned pages? If live streaming, what are your average concurrent viewers?

  * Have you partnered with any other social casinos before? if so please share relevant stats/data.

  * What would you ideally be looking for in a partnership with Shuffle.us?

A member of our partnership will review your details and get back to you in 7 days if there is mutual interest.
