# Chat Rules and Requirements

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12076371-chat-rules-and-requirements
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

To be able to unlock chat, you need to have accumulated at least 500XP on your account!  
​  
Please ensure you follow chat rules below, violating these rules may result in a temporary or permanent mute.

  1. No harassment against other users or Shuffle Staff, including profanity, abuse, racism etc.

  2. No begging for tips, loans, rains

  3. No politics/religion discussion

  4. No sexual content

  5. No sharing of any personal information, including socials

  6. No spamming of emotes or excessive capital letters/repeated words

  7. No messages of advertising/trading/selling/buying or offering services, including bots/codes/scripts

  8. No suspicious behaviour that could be seen as potential scams

  9. No discussion of illegal activities

  10. No shortened URLs, please only submit the original URL

  11. No posting of Shuffle codes

  12. Only use the designated language of the chatroom you are in
