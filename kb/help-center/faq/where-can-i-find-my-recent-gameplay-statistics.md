# Where can I find my recent gameplay statistics?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/12075228-where-can-i-find-my-recent-gameplay-statistics
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

You can request your recent statistics by email once every 24 hours.  
​  
To find your recent statistics, first, head to the "Transactions" Page and then to "[Others](https://shuffle.us/transactions/other)".

Then, click on the "Request Statistics" button.

At the moment there is a limit of 1 request every 24 hours. We'll send a breakdown of your statistics over the last day, last week, last fortnight and last month to your email.  
​  
Please note that this does not include your lifetime statistics. To find your lifetime statistics, please email [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#d2a1a7a2a2bda0a692a1baa7b4b4beb7fca7a1)
