# Game Rules

  * Infinite decks in use

  * Dealer always stands on 17

  * Dealer peeks ace and ten

  * Double any first 2

  * Can double after split

  * One card dealt to each split ace

  * Can't hit split aces

  * No re-splitting

  * Can buy insurance into ace

  * Can buy insurance with ace
