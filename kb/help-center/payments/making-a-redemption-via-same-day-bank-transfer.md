# Making a redemption via same day bank transfer

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/13626690-making-a-redemption-via-same-day-bank-transfer
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

To redeem prizes via same-day bank transfer, your account must have completed level 2 verification. You can do so here:

<https://shuffle.us/settings/verify>

Please note that redemptions by bank transfer are subject to a 1x playthrough requirement, minimum redemption requirements and any fees associated with processing the transaction.
