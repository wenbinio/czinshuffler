# How to make a purchase with Crypto on Shuffle.us?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882068-how-to-make-a-purchase-with-crypto-on-shuffle-us
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

## **Step 1: Set Up a Crypto Account**

Before making any purchases, you’ll need to create an account with a cryptocurrency platform or exchange (such as Coinbase, Binance, or Kraken).  
​

Not sure where to start? Check out our guide on getting started with cryptocurrency.

## **Step 2: Access the Wallet on Shuffle.us**

Once you’ve set up your crypto account and acquired some funds, head over to the **Wallet** section on Shuffle.us and select **Buy Gold Coins**.  
​  
Please note that you must have completed level 1 verification in order to make a purchase! You can do so [here](https://shuffle.us/settings/verify). You may be required to complete additional level of verification.

## **Step 3: Choose Your Bundle**

You’ll see a selection of Gold Coin bundles available for purchase. Choose the one that suits you best, keeping in mind:

  * There are bundles that include Gold Coins and **Shuffle Cash** , and bundles that include only Gold Coins. Make sure you select the correct bundle.

  * **Shuffle Cash** expires if you do not log into your account for a period of 60 days and play. 

Be sure to review the bundle details before confirming your choice.

## **Step 4: Select a Supported Cryptocurrency**

On the purchase page, you’ll find a dropdown menu to choose your preferred cryptocurrency.

**It’s essential to select both a currency and network supported by Shuffle.us.**

Refer to our [article](https://intercom.help/shuffleus/en/articles/11882069-supported-crypto-assets-and-chains) on supported cryptocurrencies and networks before proceeding. 

## **Step 5: Send the Crypto**

Once you've selected a currency, the site will display a unique purchase address for that transaction.  
​

You can transfer funds to this address in one of two ways:

  * Scan the QR code, or  
​

  * Copy and paste the address into the send/withdraw section of your external crypto platform.

If you’re unsure how to complete the transfer, we recommend contacting the support team of the platform or wallet you’re using — they’ll guide you through the steps.

## **Step 6: Wait for Confirmation**

After sending the crypto, the blockchain will need a bit of time to confirm the transaction. 

Once confirmed, your Gold Coins (and any included Shuffle Cash) will be added to your Shuffle.us balance automatically.
