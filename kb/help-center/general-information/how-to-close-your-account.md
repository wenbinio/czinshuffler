# How to close your account?

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882027-how-to-close-your-account
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

For **Account Closure** that is unrelated to gameplay, you may make a request via email to [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#3f4c4a4f4f504d4b7f4c574a5959535a114a4c).

* * *

If you would like to close your account for the purposes of exclusion, either temporary or permanent, you can do so automatically on the Shuffle Wise page [here](https://shuffle.us/shuffle-wise/self-exclusion).
