## What it is

- **Sweepstakes (social) casino** operating the dual-currency model: Gold Coins (GC, play-for-fun) + Shuffle Cash (SC, sweepstakes entries redeemable for prizes). No purchase necessary. ✅
- **Launched September 2025** (official blog announcement Sept 14, 2025). ✅ [VIP-Grinders](https://www.vip-grinders.com/shuffle-us-sweepstakes-launch/), [Ballislife](https://ballislife.com/betting/play/shuffle-us-sweepstakes-casino-launched/)
- **Casino-only** — no sportsbook (unlike sister site Shuffle.com). Full game access requires completing second-tier verification. ✅
- **No native mobile app**; mobile-responsive browser site only. 🟡
- Security/compliance surface: SSL, 2FA, published T&Cs, Sweepstakes Rules, Responsible Play Policy. ✅
