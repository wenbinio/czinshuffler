# Restricted States on Shuffle.us

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882042-restricted-states-on-shuffle-us
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

To play games on Shuffle.us, you must be both a legal resident of and physically located in a Permitted Jurisdiction. Here’s what you need to know:

At Shuffle.us, we’re committed to offering a safe and compliant experience for all players. To access and use our platform, you must be a legal resident of—and physically located in—a Permitted Jurisdiction.

The accepted states are Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii, Iowa, Kansas, Louisiana, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Mexico, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Virginia, Wisconsin, Wyoming.

If you are located outside of these jurisdictions, please do not attempt to access or use Shuffle.us. Doing so may result in the forfeiture of any account balances and the deactivation of your account, as we are required to follow strict compliance protocols.

If you are a resident of a Permitted Jurisdiction but are currently travelling outside of it, we kindly ask that you wait until you return before accessing Shuffle.us.

Thank you for respecting these guidelines. They help us maintain a secure and responsible environment for all players.
