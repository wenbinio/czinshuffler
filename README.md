## TL;DR of the platform

- **Model**: dual-currency sweepstakes casino — Gold Coins (fun, no value) + Shuffle Cash (1 SC ≈ $1, redeemable at 100 SC minimum after 1x playthrough).
- **Operator**: Munyon Canyon Ltd (BVI); sister brand of Shuffle.com (Natural Nine B.V., Curaçao-licensed crypto casino).
- **Launch**: September 2025. Casino-only (no sportsbook), ~500–600 games, ~15 providers, in-house Originals.
- **Redemptions**: crypto payouts (19+ coins), Level-2 KYC required, ~$100 minimum.
- **Signup offer**: 25,000 GC + 1 SC, no purchase necessary.
- **Availability**: most US states; roughly 18–19 excluded (exact list conflicts across sources — see legal file).

- Every fact carries a confidence marker: **✅ confirmed** (multiple independent sources), **🟡 likely** (single source), **❓ unverified/conflicting**.
- ⚠️ **Known trap**: Shuffle.us and Shuffle.com share branding, and review sites routinely mix their stats (game counts, providers, affiliate terms). Claims are flagged where conflation is suspected.
