# Weekly Bonuses – everything you need to know

> Source: https://help.shuffle.us/en/articles/11882124-weekly-bonuses-everything-you-need-to-know
> Fetched: 2026-07-07 (verbatim mirror, converted to markdown)

The weekly bonus is issued to eligible players every **Thursday at 3PM UTC** starting from **Bronze 1**.  
​

Bonuses appear on your **[VIP Page](https://shuffle.us/vip-program)**. If you don’t see one there, it may mean you're not currently eligible.  
​  
​**When does the weekly bonus expire?**

The weekly bonus expires after the next weekly bonus has been issued. This means you have a 7-day window to claim your bonus before it expires and a new bonus is issued.
